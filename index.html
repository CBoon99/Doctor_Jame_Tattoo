About Doctor James

Master Tattoo Artist

Located in the heart of Phuket, Doctor James Tattoo Studio has been creating exceptional body art for over a decade. Dr. James is renowned throughout Thailand and internationally for his incredible attention to detail and artistic mastery.

With expertise spanning traditional Thai designs, intricate script work, authentic bamboo tattooing, hyper-realistic portraits, and completely custom pieces, Doctor James transforms skin into stunning works of art.

Every tattoo tells a story, and Doctor James ensures that your story is told with unparalleled artistry, precision, and care. Using only the finest equipment and maintaining the highest hygiene standards, your safety and satisfaction are always guaranteed.

Tattoo Services

🐉

Traditional Tattoos

Authentic traditional designs including Thai Sak Yant, Japanese irezumi, American traditional, and tribal patterns. Each piece honors the rich history and cultural significance of traditional tattooing.

✍️

Script & Lettering

Beautiful calligraphy, meaningful quotes, names, and text in various languages and fonts. From elegant cursive to bold block letters, every word is crafted with artistic precision.

🎋

Bamboo Tattoos

Traditional hand-poked bamboo tattooing (Sak Yant) using authentic techniques passed down through generations. Experience the spiritual and cultural significance of this ancient art form.

👥

Portrait Tattoos

Hyper-realistic portraits that capture every detail and emotion. Whether it's a loved one, pet, or celebrity, these tattoos are masterpieces that look like photographs on skin.

🎨

Custom Designs

Completely original artwork created specifically for you. From concept to completion, work directly with Doctor James to create a unique piece that perfectly represents your vision.

🔧

Cover-ups & Touch-ups

Transform old or faded tattoos into stunning new artwork. Expert cover-up techniques and professional touch-up services to restore your tattoos to their original glory.
